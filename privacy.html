Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nakanishi Consulting LLC operating as Knight Fit ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our website, forms, and communications, including text messages and email.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, phone number, email address, and any other details you submit through our forms or booking pages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to schedule and confirm appointments, send reminders and follow-ups, respond to inquiries, and provide updates regarding our programs and services.

3. SMS and Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number and opting in through our forms, you may consent to receive text messages from Nakanishi Consulting LLC. We send two categories of SMS communications:

Non-Marketing Messages: These include appointment confirmations, booking reminders, call follow-ups, onboarding instructions, and program logistics. These messages are transactional in nature and are sent to fulfill the service you requested.

Marketing Messages: With your explicit opt-in consent, we may send promotional messages including new program announcements, limited-time offers, and coaching resources. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP. Consent to receive marketing messages is not a condition of purchase or participation in any program.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe at any time.

4. Email Communications

We may send you emails related to your inquiry, booking, or program enrollment. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in each email.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist in operating our website and delivering our services, under strict confidentiality agreements.

7. Mobile Information Non-Sharing Policy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information collected via SMS opt-in will not be shared with any third party for any purpose. This policy applies to all SMS programs operated by Nakanishi Consulting LLC.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and tracking technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors interact with our pages and to improve our advertising. You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us at the information below.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
